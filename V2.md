## V2 branch rule

All V2 work targets `v2` or short-lived branches created from `v2` and merged back into it.

Do not merge V2 into `main` until the Definition of Done passes, including:

- V1 history preserved and reconciled;
- bankroll reconciled;
- OpenClaw-native Sabi Boy working;
- broad multi-sport research working;
- free-first research working;
- Ticket Workshop working;
- supported bookmaker conversions working;
- price comparison/arbitrage working;
- settlement reliable;
- read-only dashboard complete;
- Sabi Boy blog complete;
- local acceptance suite passing;
- backup/restore tested;
- migration rehearsed and reversible.

## Product shorthand

**Sabi Boy does the thinking and work. The dashboard records the evidence, decisions, results and lessons.**

Sabi Boy researches sports, teams, players and context; understands bookmaker markets; reads/extracts tickets from booking codes, screenshots, social posts, copied text and instructions; edits/splits/trims/rebuilds tickets; compares prices/risk; keeps our records; learns from history; and speaks plainly with explicit selections and decimal odds.

The dashboard stays read-only and visualizes our own history, tickets, bankroll, performance, risk, model/source/system health and Sabi Boy's blog.
