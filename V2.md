# SabiAI V2

`v2` is the long-lived development branch for SabiAI V2.

`main` remains stable until V2 passes the full release gate.

1. **Product Bible** — [`docs/SABIAI_V2_PRODUCT_BIBLE.md`](docs/SABIAI_V2_PRODUCT_BIBLE.md)  
   Defines what SabiAI V2 is, its behavior, product boundaries, OpenClaw role, sports breadth, research doctrine, Ticket Workshop, bookmaker intelligence, read-only dashboard and blog.

2. **Build Plan** — [`docs/SABIAI_V2_BUILD_PLAN.md`](docs/SABIAI_V2_BUILD_PLAN.md)  
   Defines architecture, build phases, migration plan, testing, milestones, release gates and V2 Definition of Done.

3. **Living Task Board** — [`docs/SABIAI_V2_TASKS.md`](docs/SABIAI_V2_TASKS.md)  
   Tracks implementation progress. Update it continuously during the build.

## V2 branch rule

All V2 work targets `v2` (or short-lived feature/fix branches created from `v2` and merged back into it).

Do not merge V2 into `main` until the V2 Definition of Done passes, including:

- V1 history preserved and reconciled;
- bankroll reconciled;
- OpenClaw-native Sabi working;
- broad multi-sport research working;
- free-first research working;
- Ticket Workshop working;
- supported bookmaker conversions working;
- odds comparison/arbitrage working;
- settlement reliable;
- read-only dashboard complete;
- Sabi blog complete;
- local acceptance suite passing;
- backup/restore tested;
- migration rehearsed and reversible.

## Product shorthand

**OpenClaw Sabi does the work. The dashboard records our story.**

Sabi researches sports, teams, players, form, injuries, H2H, markets and bookmakers; reads booking codes, screenshots, social posts and instructions; edits, splits, trims, converts and rebuilds tickets; and speaks in plain language with decimal odds.

The dashboard stays read-only and visualizes only our own history, games, tickets, streaks, bankroll, strategies, performance and Sabi's blog.
